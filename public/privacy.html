Privacy

Your CV never leaves your device. Libre CV is a static website: reading your PDF, OCR, editing and exporting all happen inside your browser. There is no server-side code, no database, no account, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.

Your CV is saved in your browser's local storage so it is still there when you come back. Use Clear in the app (or clear this site's data in your browser) to delete it.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the pages and protect against abuse; we do not use Cloudflare analytics and receive no personal data.

The code is open source (MIT): github.com/guipguia/libre-cv.
